Having you as my sister is one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

We've shared laughter, silly fights, unforgettable moments,
secrets, and countless memories.

Sometimes I may annoy you.
Sometimes we may fight.
Sometimes we may not agree.

But underneath all of that, there is something that will never change:

You will always be my sister,
and you will always have a special place in my heart. ❤️

I hope this birthday brings you happiness that stays,
dreams that come true,
and memories that you'll treasure forever.

Never forget how loved you are.

Happy Birthday, Sis. ❤️
